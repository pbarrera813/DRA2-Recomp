Digimon Rumble Arena 2 - native PC port (built from your own disc)

Play: start "Digimon Rumble Arena 2" from the Start menu.
Controllers: up to four; controller 1 is player 1, with rumble. Tested:
DualSense and Xbox Series X|S over USB or Bluetooth, Switch 2 Pro over USB.
Switch 2 Pro: close Steam completely first, or the game cannot see it.
Display: starts in borderless fullscreen at 1080p, widened to fill 16:9
screens; F11 or Alt+Enter switches to a window. Alt+F4 quits.
Menu: the PS / Xbox / Home button, or Back + Start held for a second, opens
Resolution, Widescreen, Fullscreen, Monitor and Quit. On the title screen
(PRESS START), Esc quits too.
Resolution (1080/1440/2160), widescreen (0 = original 4:3), fullscreen and
monitor are in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\DigimonRumbleArena2\settings.ini (created on first start).
Saves: %LOCALAPPDATA%\DigimonRumbleArena2.

This copy contains game data from your own disc. Do not share it.

Third-party libraries: SDL3 3.4.18 (zlib license) and libusb 1.0.30 (LGPL 2.1,
source at https://github.com/libusb/libusb/releases/tag/v1.0.30); their
licenses are in the licenses folder.
